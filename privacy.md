### Summary

- **Your financial data stays on your iPhone.** Transactions, income, expenses, recurring bills, categories, notes, and payday settings are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Pokko servers.
- **Pokko does not use advertising, tracking, analytics, or data brokers.** We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.
- **Purchases are processed by Apple, with RevenueCat used for subscription management.** RevenueCat uses an anonymous App User ID to validate purchases, restore purchases, and check subscription status.
- **You control your local data.** You can delete it with **Settings → Reset data** in Pokko or by deleting the app. Because Pokko does not keep a server copy, we cannot recover deleted local data.

### Information We Collect

#### Financial information stored on your device

Pokko lets you enter information needed to manage your personal money, including:

- income and expense transactions;
- transaction amounts;
- categories;
- notes;
- recurring bills; and
- payday and related spending settings.

This information is stored only on your device in a local database encrypted at rest. Pokko has no server containing this information, so the developer cannot view, access, or recover your financial records.

Pokko does not currently provide cloud synchronization or cloud backup.

#### Purchase information

If you buy Pokko PRO, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. Pokko does not receive your payment card information.

Pokko uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore purchases, and check subscription status. The RevenueCat SDK is configured with an API key and uses an anonymous App User ID generated by RevenueCat when no custom user ID is provided. Pokko does not use an account, custom identifying user ID, or subscriber attributes such as email, name, or phone number.

For operating its service, RevenueCat may process technical and transaction information associated with requests, including the anonymous App User ID, App Store receipt or transaction information, subscription status, app version, operating system version, storefront country, and IP address. RevenueCat describes its handling of end-user technical and transaction information in its [Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

Pokko does not send your financial transactions, amounts, notes, categories, recurring bills, or payday settings to RevenueCat.

### Information We Don't Collect

Pokko does not:

- require an account or sign-up;
- collect your name, email address, or phone number for an account;
- upload your financial records to Pokko servers;
- provide cloud backup or synchronization in this version;
- use analytics SDKs;
- use advertising SDKs;
- use crash-reporting SDKs;
- use advertising or attribution identifiers;
- track you across apps or websites;
- sell personal data;
- share personal data with data brokers;
- send your financial information to RevenueCat;
- use RevenueCat Paywalls or RevenueCat paywall analytics events; or
- set RevenueCat subscriber attributes such as email, name, or phone number.

Pokko does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission because Pokko does not engage in tracking.

### How Your Data Is Stored

Your financial data is stored locally on your iPhone in a database encrypted at rest.

Pokko does not maintain a server copy of this information and does not provide cloud sync or backup in the current version.

Widgets read the information they need from local on-device data and display a summary on the Home Screen or Lock Screen. Widget data is not uploaded to a Pokko server.

### Subscriptions & Payments

Pokko PRO is available as an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) or as a one-time Lifetime purchase.

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Pokko does not receive or store your payment card details.

Before you buy, the App Store purchase flow shows the applicable price, billing period, and any introductory or promotional offer. An auto-renewable subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it through Apple.

You can manage or cancel your subscription through:

**iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**

Pokko also provides a **Restore Purchases** option in the app.

A subscription provides access to the premium features described in the purchase flow for the applicable subscription period. The Lifetime purchase provides ongoing access to those features without renewal. Prices and available features may change for future purchases, subject to applicable App Store rules.

Apple's standard EULA applies to Pokko purchases: [Apple Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).

### Third-Party Services (Apple, RevenueCat)

#### Apple

Apple provides the App Store and payment processing for Pokko purchases. Apple processes payment information under Apple's own terms and privacy practices. Pokko does not receive your payment card details.

Apple also provides iOS services used by Pokko, including Face ID authentication, the Keychain, local notifications, widgets, and App Store purchase services.

#### RevenueCat

[RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

Pokko uses RevenueCat only for subscription-management functions: validating purchases, restoring purchases, and checking subscription status.

Pokko uses RevenueCat's anonymous App User ID. We do not log users in to RevenueCat with a custom or identifying user ID and do not send subscriber attributes such as email, name, or phone number.

RevenueCat may receive technical and transaction information needed to operate its service, such as the anonymous App User ID, App Store receipt or transaction information, subscription status, app version, operating system version, storefront country, and IP address. Pokko does not use this information to identify you, and Pokko does not use RevenueCat for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Pokko does not enable RevenueCat device-identifier collection or advertising/attribution integrations, does not use RevenueCat Paywalls, and does not send financial data to RevenueCat.

### Permissions (Face ID, Notifications)

#### Face ID / PIN

Locking Pokko with Face ID or a PIN is optional.

Face ID authentication is handled by Apple's iOS security framework. Pokko receives only the result of the authentication attempt, such as success or failure. Pokko does not receive, store, or access your Face ID biometric data.

If you set a Pokko PIN, only a salted, one-way hash of it is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. It is never sent anywhere. Keychain items may remain on the device after the app is deleted until they are replaced or removed by iOS.

#### Notifications

If you allow notifications, Pokko can schedule daily spending reminders locally on your device.

Pokko does not operate a push notification server and does not send a device push token to its servers for these reminders.

You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

### Data Export

Pokko lets you export your financial data when you choose to do so.

The export file, such as a CSV or Excel file, is created on your device. Pokko does not automatically upload or send the exported file anywhere.

After export, you decide where to save or share the file. The information in an exported file may therefore be accessible to the person or service you choose to share it with.

Depending on applicable law, including Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on Personal Data Protection, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including the right to:

- know about the processing of your personal data;
- access your personal data;
- request correction of inaccurate personal data;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- request deletion of personal data, subject to applicable legal exceptions;
- request restriction of processing where applicable;
- request provision of personal data where applicable;
- object to certain processing where applicable;
- make a complaint or exercise other legal rights; and
- request compensation where provided by applicable law.

For Pokko's local financial data, you can normally exercise control directly on your device because Pokko does not keep a server copy.

For data processed by Apple or RevenueCat, their own privacy policies and applicable legal processes also apply.

### Data Retention & Deletion

#### Pokko local data

Your financial data remains on your device until you use **Settings → Reset data** in Pokko or delete the app. Reset data permanently deletes all transactions, recurring expenses, and settings.

Deleting the app removes Pokko's local database from the device. Because Pokko does not maintain a server copy or cloud backup, the developer cannot recover deleted local data.

#### RevenueCat and purchase records

RevenueCat retains end-user technical and transaction information according to its applicable retention practices and legal obligations. Apple separately retains App Store transaction and subscription records under Apple's policies and applicable requirements.

You may contact Pokko to request deletion of a RevenueCat record associated with your anonymous App User ID. Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or transaction-related reasons.

See the [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) for its current privacy and retention practices.

### Children's Privacy

Pokko is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to Pokko through a service we control, please contact us.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Pokko's features, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

If we make a material change, we will announce it in the app or on this page and update the **Effective date** and **Last updated** information above.

We will update this policy before introducing features such as cloud backup, account sign-in, synchronization, analytics, or other material changes to how data is handled.

### Quyền truy cập (Face ID, Thông báo)

#### Face ID / Mã PIN

Bạn có thể tùy chọn khóa Pokko bằng Face ID hoặc mã PIN.

Việc xác thực Face ID được thực hiện bởi hệ thống bảo mật của iOS. Pokko chỉ nhận kết quả của lần xác thực, chẳng hạn thành công hoặc thất bại. Pokko không nhận, lưu trữ hoặc truy cập dữ liệu sinh trắc học Face ID.

Nếu bạn đặt mã PIN cho Pokko, chỉ bản băm một chiều (có thêm muối) của mã PIN được lưu trong Chuỗi khóa (Keychain) của iOS trên thiết bị. Mã PIN không bao giờ được gửi đi. Các mục trong Chuỗi khóa có thể vẫn còn trên thiết bị sau khi xóa ứng dụng cho đến khi được thay thế hoặc được iOS xóa.

#### Thông báo

Nếu bạn cho phép thông báo, Pokko có thể lên lịch các lời nhắc chi tiêu hằng ngày trực tiếp trên thiết bị.

Pokko không vận hành máy chủ thông báo đẩy và không gửi mã thông báo đẩy (push token) của thiết bị đến máy chủ của mình cho các lời nhắc này.

Bạn có thể tắt thông báo bất kỳ lúc nào trong Cài đặt iOS.
